# Good Tidings Bible

The King James Version in plain, modern American English, with notes on the original Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek.
Named from Isaiah 61:1, "to preach good tidings unto the meek", the passage Jesus read at Nazareth as His own mission (Luke 4:18-21).

**Read it:** https://arsiud.github.io/good-tidings/ (works on any phone or computer; add it to your home screen to use it like an app, offline too).

## Suggestions welcome

The translation is new and being checked verse by verse. If a verse reads wrong, is unclear, or misses the meaning:

1. Open the app and **hold the verse** (tap more verses to add them).
2. Tap **Suggest**. A form opens here with the verses and their current wording filled in.
3. Write your wording and why, and submit.

Suggestions on the notes and general feedback work the same way (the app's **Feedback** tab). You can also [open an issue](https://github.com/Arsiud/good-tidings/issues/new/choose) directly. Accepted suggestions go into the translation itself, so everyone gets the better wording.
