# Good Tidings Bible: terms of use

Copyright © 2026 Yuriy Lashchuk. The Good Tidings Bible (GTB), its text and its notes, is given freely
for everyone to read and share. "Freely you have received; freely give." (Matthew 10:8)

## Free, no permission needed

You may, without asking:

- read, copy, print, quote and share it, in part or in full;
- put it in an app, website, e-book, audio recording, slide, church bulletin, study or teaching
  material, as long as people get the translation free of charge;
- show ads, accept donations or have sponsors on a free app or website that includes it, as long as
  no one pays for the translation itself.

Changing the layout, font, formatting or which passages you show is fine.

## Permission needed

Ask first, in writing, before you:

- **sell it or make money from the translation itself**: printed Bibles or books for sale, paid apps,
  paid downloads or recordings, subscriptions or paywalls that include the text, or any product whose
  revenue comes from the translation;
- **change it**: publish the text or notes with words altered, added or removed, present altered text
  as the Good Tidings Bible, or make a new translation or paraphrase based on it.

## Corrections and suggestions

Changes are welcome as suggestions. If you find a mistake or have a better rendering, send it in;
suggestions that are accepted go into the Good Tidings Bible itself, so everyone gets the correction.

## Contact

Permission requests and suggestions: open an issue at https://github.com/Arsiud/good-tidings/issues
(the app's Suggest button fills one in for you).
